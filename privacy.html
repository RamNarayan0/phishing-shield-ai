Privacy Policy for Phishing Shield AI

Last Updated: October 2026

Overview

Phishing Shield AI is a cybersecurity tool designed to detect phishing attacks, malicious links, and scam communications in real time.

Data Collection and Usage

Phishing Shield AI only processes text and URLs that you explicitly choose to scan:

  • Selected Text & URLs: When you trigger a scan via the Chrome extension or web dashboard, the selected message or URL is sent securely to our threat analysis engine for pattern matching and risk scoring.
  • No Storage of Personal Data: We do not sell, store, or profile your personal data, passwords, or personal credentials.
  • Local Settings: Your preferred backend API selection is stored locally in your browser's chrome.storage.local or localStorage.

Permissions

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly to read the text you have highlighted on a webpage when you click "Scan Selected Message".
  • storage: Used to remember your preferred backend connection mode.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy or Phishing Shield AI, visit our GitHub Repository.
